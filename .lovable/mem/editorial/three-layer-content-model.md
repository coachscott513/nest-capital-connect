Every business page has THREE content layers. Never blur them.

**Layer 1 — Verified Public Info (publish immediately):**
Address, phone, hours, website, reservation links, menu highlights, services, cuisine, private events, outdoor dining, gift cards, FAQs, official photos (if terms permit), Instagram embeds, Facebook links. Always rewrite in CDN's own editorial voice — never copy marketing copy verbatim.

**Layer 2 — CDN Editorial (our value-add):**
Original observations. "First-Time Visitors" tips, "Atmosphere" summary, "What They're Known For" (never "best" — use "Regular guests often mention: …"). This is curation, not fabrication — only include qualities visible in real reviews/menus/photos.

**Layer 3 — Owner Verified (gold standard):**
Owner story, chef story, real team members, history, philosophy, seasonal updates, original photography, video, events. Only after owner responds to questionnaire. Unlocks the "Owner Verified" badge.

**Team pages — hard rule:** Never invent people. If the website lists names, include them. Otherwise show a generic "supported by a talented team" line + "Owner information coming soon" — or omit the section.

**Menus:** Summarize categories (steaks, seafood, cocktails, seasonal). Never copy menu descriptions verbatim.

**Badge system** (render on every business page):
- 🟢 Owner Verified
- 🔵 Capital District Nest Editorial
- 🟣 Community Favorite
- 🟡 Seasonally Updated
- 📷 Original Photography
- 🎥 Video Available

**Workflow:** Assistant builds ~80% of every page from public sources BEFORE contacting the business. Questionnaire becomes a verification/enhancement step, not a blank form. Outreach message: "We've already created your Capital District Nest profile using publicly available information. We'd love your help refining it…"
