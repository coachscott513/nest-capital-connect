## Requirements

**Search scope truthfulness**
- The principal property-search module must state prominently that it searches
  across the Capital District. It must never imply City of Albany only.
- The Albany-specific RealScout saved-map link may remain as a secondary
  entry, but must be labeled truthfully as Albany-scoped.

**Closing Team role cards**
- Fully clickable; each leads to a neutral role/category destination.
- A generic role card never sends a user directly to a single paid provider.
- A named-provider/partner rail is allowed only once a readiness threshold is
  met. The threshold must be explicit (a stated minimum of verified providers
  per role) so the module is never publicly empty.

**Property Intelligence cards**
- Fully clickable, each with one clear primary destination.

**Neutrality and honesty**
- Paid placement never changes organic rank.
- Sponsored Spotlight is clearly labeled and visually separate from neutral
  ranking.
- No fake providers, fake counts, empty inventory, or placeholder cards. A
  module that cannot be filled truthfully does not ship.

**Contracts to preserve**
- All indexed business, town, and property URL contracts remain unchanged.
- Existing canonical, sitemap, and prerender behavior remains unchanged.

**Cross-product design relationship**
- Shared AnalyzeAnyProperty typography, spacing, controls, and evidence
  semantics may inform the ecosystem.
- Capital District Nest retains its premium dark regional identity.
- AnalyzeAnyProperty retains its documentary paper/survey/evidence-report
  mode. The two products are not forced into one identical color treatment.
