What It's Like Living in Cohoes, NY

Cohoes pairs riverfront character and a walkable downtown with some of the most affordable real estate in the Capital District. A quietly growing arts and dining scene anchors the city.

Things to Do in Cohoes, NY

  • Cohoes Music Hall (1874) — concerts, comedy, theater
  • Cohoes Falls overlook park
  • Riverfront walking and bike paths
  • Downtown Remsen Street dining

Schools in Cohoes, NY

Cohoes is served by the Cohoes City School District, with one elementary, middle, and high school in a small, tight-knit district.

Commute from Cohoes to downtown Albany

Cohoes is 12–18 minutes from downtown Albany via I-787 along the Hudson, and 5 minutes from Latham, Watervliet, and Troy.

What's Happening in Cohoes, NY

Downtown Cohoes continues to add restaurants and arts venues, with the Music Hall and Falls overlook anchoring growing visitor traffic.
