Schools in Colonie, NY

Colonie is served by either the South Colonie or North Colonie school district depending on neighborhood, both consistently ranked among the strongest in the Capital Region.

Commute from Colonie to downtown Albany

Most of Colonie is 10–15 minutes from downtown Albany via I-87 or Wolf Road, with quick access to Schenectady, Saratoga, and the airport.

What's Happening in Colonie, NY

Colonie continues to expand its retail and dining footprint along Wolf Road and around the Crossings, while school quality and central location keep family demand steady.
