What It's Like Living in Latham, NY

Living in Latham is convenience-first: airport in 5 minutes, downtown Albany in 15, and Saratoga in 20. Family neighborhoods, walking trails, and the Crossings park anchor the local scene.

Things to Do in Latham, NY

  • Concerts and festivals at the Crossings of Colonie
  • Mohawk-Hudson bike-hike trail
  • Shopping and dining along Wolf Road
  • Saratoga Race Course in 25 minutes
  • Albany Pine Bush Preserve

Schools in Latham, NY

Latham is served by the North Colonie Central School District, one of the top-rated districts in the Capital Region with strong academics and athletics. Shaker High School consistently ranks among the best in upstate NY.

Commute from Latham to downtown Albany

Latham is 10–15 minutes from downtown Albany via I-87, 15 minutes to Schenectady via Route 7, and 25 minutes to Saratoga Springs via the Northway.

What's Happening in Latham, NY

Latham continues to add new restaurants and retail along Wolf Road and around the Crossings, while remaining one of the most stable family markets in the Capital Region.
