Watervliet is a small, working-class Hudson River city that punches above its weight in affordability and access. Hudson Shores Park anchors a growing riverfront identity.

Schools in Watervliet, NY

Watervliet is served by the Watervliet City School District, a single small district covering the entire city.

Commute from Watervliet to downtown Albany

Watervliet is 8–12 minutes from downtown Albany via I-787 along the Hudson, with Troy directly across the river via the Congress Street Bridge.
